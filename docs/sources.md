## Recorded

### Extension-worker ratio
Cited: below 1:5000 actual, against a national guideline of 1:1100 irrigated,
1:750 rainfed, 1:400 hilly. Source: ICRISAT,
https://pressroom.icrisat.org/strengthening-agri-extension-for-a-strong-farm-sector

Alternative framing, to be stated alongside and not instead of the above:
other sources give 1:1162 actual against 1:750 recommended. The two are not
contradictory — they use different denominators, one per operational holding
and one per farmer. Cite ICRISAT; note both. Never collapse to one number.
